## Backlog Issues

## Post-Demo Campaign Expansion

### Expand campaign biome roster

**Milestone:** Post-Demo Campaign Expansion
**Status:** Planned
**Priority:** High

#### Problem
After the first Pig Kin demo, the game needs to grow from the opening arc into a full campaign of roughly ten biomes before the final Library encounter.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- A biome expansion plan defines candidate biome order, enemy families, bosses, map-generation needs, story role, and progression rewards.
- Each promoted biome receives a feature-level implementation issue before work begins.
- Existing demo biomes remain valid early-game content instead of being overwritten by later balance.

### Add additional kin lineages

**Milestone:** Post-Demo Campaign Expansion
**Status:** Planned
**Priority:** High

#### Problem
Pig Kin is the first reconstruction target, but the broader game needs multiple kin lineages with distinct identity, materials, and balance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- Each new kin has material and catalyst sources protected from random progression failure.
- Wrong Machine reconstruction supports the lineage with clear eligibility and result presentation.
- Random recruitment and reward pools respect owned-lineage gates.
- Kin passives or stat identities are balanced against Basic Goblin and Pig Kin.

### Expand affix and dice progression

**Milestone:** Post-Demo Campaign Expansion
**Status:** Planned
**Priority:** Medium

#### Problem
The post-demo game needs broader dice and affix progression without making early dice or basic builds obsolete too quickly.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- Additional affixes are designed with role, rarity, and combat-math constraints.
- Dice progression supports later biome difficulty while preserving readable choices.
- Salvage, Raw Chaos, and future fabrication rules are balanced against new affix availability.

### Design final Library encounter arc

**Milestone:** Post-Demo Campaign Expansion
**Status:** Planned
**Priority:** Medium

#### Problem
The Library is the eventual campaign capstone, but it should be designed after enough biome, kin, and progression breadth exists to support a satisfying finale.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- The Library arc defines story stakes, enemy identity, required progression, final encounter rules, and post-victory state.
- Prerequisite biome and kin work is identified before implementation begins.
- The finale preserves repeatable post-campaign play where appropriate.
