## Post-Demo Campaign Expansion

**Status:** Planned

After the first Pig Kin demo release, expand Dice Goblins from the opening demo arc into the broader campaign: more biomes, more kin, more affixes, richer progression, and the final Library encounter.

Success criteria:

- The campaign grows toward roughly ten meaningful biomes before the final Library encounter.
- Each new biome adds distinct enemies, map patterns, materials, dialogue, hazards, shrines, chaos entries, rewards, and boss identity.
- Additional kin lineages have protected material/catalyst paths, Wrong Machine reconstruction flows, and balance validation.
- Affix and dice progression grows without invalidating early-game choices.
- The final Library encounter is designed as the campaign capstone after enough biome and lineage breadth exists.
