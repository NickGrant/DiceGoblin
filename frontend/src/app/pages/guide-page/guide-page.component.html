How To Play

Build a squad, equip dice, survive the route, spend the loot.

Dice Goblins is a squad-building run game. Most decisions happen before and after expeditions: who joins the squad, where they stand, which dice power their abilities, and what upgrades you buy before the next attempt.

Public Guide
  1. Assemble a squad with a front row that can hold and a back row that can finish fights.
  2. Equip dice to the abilities that matter most for your plan.
  3. Choose a region, push through nodes, then return home with rewards and upgrades.
