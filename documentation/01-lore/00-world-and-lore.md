# Dice Goblins - World and Lore

## World
Dice Goblins takes place in a bright, apparently prosperous world stabilized by Order. The Archivist and the Grand Library represent classification, continuity, safety, and the suppression of unstable possibilities.

Chaos and Order are not simple good/evil alignments. Order makes civilization safer and more predictable, while excessive Order suppresses change and anything that cannot be made stable. Chaos permits possibility, improvisation, mutation, accidents, and unstable creation, while excessive Chaos makes lasting structures difficult.

## Goblins and The Whim
Goblins are physical manifestations/fragments of The Whim, the closest thing the setting has to a deity of Chaos. The player is a goblin warchief rebuilding goblinkind from inside goblin values rather than a conventional hero protecting civilization.

Goblins should feel mischievous, scavenging, impulsive, ambitious, funny, disposable, and dangerous. The world has legitimate reasons to fear their return.

## The Archivist and Grand Library
The Archivist is the champion of Order. Historical goblinkind was eradicated through direct violence and forced stabilization. The Grand Library's cataloging and magic help define which versions of reality are considered stable and permitted.

The Archivist should not be reduced to a cartoon villain. Order has produced real benefits for many inhabitants; the conflict becomes existential because true goblin chaos cannot be fully classified or contained.

## Dice
Dice are physical shards of chaos power rather than merely UI abstractions. Goblins collect and bind them to abilities because they preserve condensed pieces of suppressed possibility.

Mechanical dice identity is defined by the vNext profile/material/aspect system; lore should not imply that material, rarity, or any legacy affix model is the sole identity of a die.

## Teeth and Raw Chaos
Teeth are ordinary monster currency used in goblin commerce.

Raw Chaos is goblin essence/chaos power released or recovered from stabilized forms. It is scarce and is associated with changing what the player is capable of, especially restoration/reconstruction. It is not merely a rarer version of Teeth.

## The Wrong Machine
The Wrong Machine is an ancient chaos-working device. Its name is a proper name, not proof that it is broken. It can reconstruct specific goblins/kin from Raw Chaos and is an intentional repeatable Raw Chaos sink once available.

Specific custody history, unlock scene, recipes, and biome story beats are intentionally not fixed in this lore baseline; they should be authored alongside the vNext milestones that implement them.

## Kin
Kin are goblin forms associated with traits recovered from creature families that Order stabilized away from goblinkind. Restoring kin returns chaotic possibility to goblins; it is not framed as modern genetic experimentation.

## Long-Term Objective
The player's growing warband gathers dice, restores goblin possibilities, disrupts imposed Order, and ultimately challenges The Archivist. The world should remain morally interesting: the player is restoring something that was violently suppressed, but is also deliberately reintroducing dangerous instability.
