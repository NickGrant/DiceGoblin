# Character Profiles and Dialogue Voices

These profiles define durable voice/motivation. They intentionally avoid locking dialogue sequencing or partially implemented story-state machinery.

## Player Goblin
A newly manifested goblin who becomes warchief of a restored people. Immediate, concrete, curious, greedy, destructive, and instinctively resistant to authority. Dialogue should use internally consistent goblin logic, not random nonsense or modern quips. Common voice lanes are violence, greed, and literal/opportunistic goblin logic.

## The Whim
Chaos-aligned patron/source of goblins. Calm, amused, lightly distant, and hostile to the premise of a controlled plan. Gives enough information to provoke action but not enough to make it safe. Treats unexpected consequences as evidence that possibility still exists. Irresponsible rather than sadistic.

## The Archivist
Champion of Order. Measured, precise, restrained, and authoritative. Believes safety requires classification and containment and initially treats the player as an error requiring correction rather than a personal enemy. Violence is framed as preservation/correction rather than cruelty.

## Tooth Collector
Persistent merchant associated with Teeth. Quiet, transactional, unsettling, and useful. Answers practical commercial questions while evading personal ones. Their precise origin and any larger purpose remain deliberately unresolved unless later narrative work explicitly settles them.

## Mudking
Farm boss. Dumb, pompous, territorial, and sincerely convinced ordinary mud and broken farm structures are royal accomplishments. Grandiose language, misused formality, and absolute confidence; should remain threatening enough to function as the first boss.

## Kobold Chief Engineer
Mountain boss identity. Technical, curious, procedural, skittery under pressure, and legitimately knowledgeable. Uses precise terminology and reframes mistakes as test results. Specific Wrong Machine custody/confession beats from the prototype are not canonical vNext requirements unless reauthored later.

## Bog Tyrant
Swamp boss identity. Loud, blunt, dominant, and pragmatic. Understands authority physically and directly. Uses simple commands and threats rather than subtle political speeches. Specific prototype Wrong Machine custody/recovery beats are not canonical vNext requirements unless reauthored later.

## Voice Contrast
| Character | Uncertainty | Authority | Rhythm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Player | acts into it | rejects it | short/reactive |
| Whim | celebrates it | evades responsibility | calm/suggestive |
| Archivist | eliminates it | embodies it | measured/formal |
| Tooth Collector | profits from it | works beside it | quiet/transactional |
| Mudking | barely notices it | invents grandeur | pompous/confused |
| Chief Engineer | studies it | procedural | fast/technical |
| Bog Tyrant | hits it | uses force | loud/direct |
