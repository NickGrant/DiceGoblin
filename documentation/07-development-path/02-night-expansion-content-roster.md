# Night Expansion Biome, Enemy, and Kin Roster

## Purpose

Define the approved high-level content roster for the first Dice Goblins expansion, **Night**.

This document is canonical for expansion allocation and the biome-family-kin pairings. It does not make these entries current content, part of the base-game release, or eligible for runtime generation. Complete content packages must later be promoted into the relevant catalogs under `documentation/03-content/`.

## Expansion Roster

| Order | Biome | Native enemy family | Associated kin | Planning notes |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ruins | Raccoons | Raccoon Kin | Nocturnal thieves living among the remains of an older civilization. They are forced to catalogue and preserve artifacts they instinctively want to dismantle or hoard. |
| 2 | Meadow | Moths | Moth Kin | Dusty nocturnal creatures attracted to fire, magic, and bright objects. Order has made them lantern keepers and signal-tower attendants. |
| 3 | Cemetery | Crows | Crow Kin | Clever scavengers that collect jewelry, bones, and unattended possessions. Order has turned them into undertakers, grave wardens, and custodians of the dead. |

The listed order is the approved working expansion sequence. Detailed campaign placement, access requirements, and progression dependencies remain future design work.

## Expansion Identity

Night focuses on creatures whose scavenging instincts, nocturnal behavior, and relationship with discarded objects have been redirected into rigid maintenance roles by Order.

Shared thematic tensions include:

- hoarding versus cataloguing;
- attraction and curiosity versus assigned duty;
- scavenging the dead versus preserving official memory;
- nocturnal freedom versus scheduled custodial work;
- dismantling and repurposing versus maintaining an approved record.

These themes should connect the expansion to the base game's conflict with The Library without requiring every region to be physically located near the final base-game biome.

## Release Boundary

The following are explicitly reserved for Night and excluded from the base-game roster:

- Ruins, Raccoons, and Raccoon Kin;
- Meadow, Moths, and Moth Kin;
- Cemetery, Crows, and Crow Kin.

Base-game encounter, reward, Codex, recruitment, reconstruction, and run-generation pools must not include these entries before the expansion is activated.

## Required Future Definition

Before implementation, each expansion biome requires:

- stable content keys and progression placement;
- concrete enemy unit and boss rosters;
- kin mechanics, reconstruction recipes, and traversal or combat identity;
- lineage materials and boss catalysts;
- encounter, hazard, shrine, reward, dialogue, and Codex packages;
- environment, enemy, kin, badge, and interface art;
- an expansion entitlement or activation boundary if the release model requires one.

## Relationship to Earlier Planning

Ruins is not the former Haunted Ruins chapter from the older twelve-chapter proposal. The approved Ruins biome is specifically the Raccoon region described here.

Meadow and Cemetery likewise belong to Night and must not be substituted into the base-game sequence without an explicit release-scope change.

## Maintenance Notes

- Update this document before changing expansion allocation or pairings.
- Promote entries into current content catalogs only when expansion implementation begins.
- Keep expansion content out of base-game counts and current Codex totals until activated.
