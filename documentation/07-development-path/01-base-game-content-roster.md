# Approved Base-Game Biome, Enemy, and Kin Roster

This document allocates the planned base-game biome, native enemy family, and associated kin. It does not require every entry to be implemented during the vNext technical overhaul.

| Order | Biome | Native enemy family | Associated kin |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Farm | Pigs | Pig Kin |
| 2 | Mountains | Kobolds | Lizard Kin |
| 3 | Swamps | Frogmen | Frog Kin |
| 4 | Island | Monkeys | Monkey Kin |
| 5 | Tundra | Walrusfolk | Walrus Kin |
| 6 | Volcano | Salamanders | Salamander Kin |
| 7 | Wasteland | Vultures | Vulture Kin |
| 8 | River | Otters | Otter Kin |
| 9 | Orchard | Wasps | Wasp Kin |
| 10 | Savanna | Hyenas | Hyena Kin |

## Special Biomes
- **Mystic Cave** - Chaos beings/The Whim; establishes Basic Goblin rather than an enemy-derived kin; onboarding and recurring special content.
- **The Library** - The Archivist and Order-aligned forces; final base-game confrontation; no standard enemy-derived kin currently assigned.

## vNext Boundary
The overhaul initially proves Farm, uses Mountains to prove region generalization, and later uses Swamps for repeatability/parity. Island through Savanna and The Library are future content production rather than prerequisites for completing the vNext architecture migration.

A roster allocation is not a complete runtime definition. A biome enters implementation only when its milestone authors the required JSON, enemies/encounters, rewards, progression, presentation, and assets.
